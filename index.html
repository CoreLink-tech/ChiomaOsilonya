Who She Is

Driven by purpose,
defined by service

Osilonya Chioma Peace Oghosa is a GenZ leader who graduated from the University of Benin with a Bachelor's Degree in International Studies and Diplomacy.

A multi-industry entrepreneur, she has worked with various companies and brands, bringing energy, vision, and impact wherever she goes.

She carries a deep passion for leadership, public service, and social impact. She is driven by the desire to inspire young people, advocate for civic responsibility, and contribute to national and regional development.

For Chioma, leadership is not a title. It is a commitment to show up, serve, and build bridges between where Nigeria is and where it must go.

What She Stands For

Eight pillars of real change

Chioma's vision is built on concrete priorities, empowering young Nigerians to thrive, lead, and build the country they deserve.

01

Education & Youth Empowerment

Championing quality education and opportunities that equip young Nigerians with the knowledge to lead and innovate.

02

Skill Acquisition

Promoting practical, hands-on skill programs that create pathways to economic independence for young people.

03

Civic Rights & Responsible Citizenship

Educating and mobilizing citizens to understand their rights and engage meaningfully in governance.

04

Women Empowerment & Inclusion

Breaking barriers so women can lead, participate, and thrive equally in every space.

05

Leadership Development

Nurturing the next generation of purposeful leaders through mentorship, training, and exposure.

06

Peacebuilding & National Unity

Fostering dialogue, tolerance, and shared identity across ethnic, religious, and regional divides.

07

Youth in Governance & Policy

Advocating for genuine youth representation in decision-making bodies.

08

Community Development & Social Impact

Building communities through outreach, volunteer initiatives, and sustainable development projects.
